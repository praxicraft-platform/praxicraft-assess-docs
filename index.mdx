Your team designs hiring in Assess. Candidates take assessments and interviews in the product, they never see your API keys, ATS, or automation stack.

Day-to-day work lives in the [dashboard](https://assess.praxicraft.com/assess). Connect software when you need it through the Public API, SDKs, and webhooks.

## What Assess can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Assessments" icon="clipboard-check" href="/assessments">
    Build active tests with platform or org tasks, time limits, and passing scores.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Invitations" icon="mail" href="/invitations">
    Send unique candidate links  single or bulk  with reminders and expiry.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Results" icon="chart-bar" href="/results">
    Read scores, pass/fail, and per-task outcomes for every session.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Proctoring" icon="eye" href="/proctoring">
    Integrity signals for review  they never auto-fail a candidate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live interviews" icon="video" href="/interviews">
    AI-only, human, or hybrid rooms with analysis and replay.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pipelines" icon="git-branch" href="/pipelines">
    Multi-stage funnels that advance or stop candidates by rule.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Webhooks" icon="webhook" href="/webhooks">

  </Card>
